The original evolution (v1)

EvoMaxx v1's own loop, as it was: a pool of 12 organisms; every 90 steps a mutated child takes over the living world, every 270 steps the two fittest are crossed, and the weakest leaves the pool. Fitness is v1's score of the field: its texture, colour diversity, brightness and contrast. An extinction keeps the best genome and starts a fresh pool on a fresh world.
